Initial release of Jobify:
• Browse job boards in a built-in browser that keeps your logins
• Check eligibility, red flags and fit using the AI provider you choose (Gemini, an OpenAI-compatible service, or your own Ollama/LM Studio server)
• Auto-fill application forms from your profile and saved answers
• Save jobs for later and track applications with follow-up reminders
• Insights on your application progress
• Light and dark themes, text size, page zoom and reduced motion settings
• Local JSON backup and restore
